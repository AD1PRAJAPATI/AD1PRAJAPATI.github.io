02 / Research

Research interests

Policy routing

I build systems that choose the best controller at each step, instead of asking one robot policy to solve every part of a task.

Embodied AI

I study how generalist robot models can connect language and visual perception to reliable, grounded action.

Robustness

I develop failure detectors and safety probes that identify when a robot’s view, instruction, or environment has shifted.

Sim-to-real transfer

I use targeted contrast sets and simulation data to test what actually transfers before a model reaches a real robot.
